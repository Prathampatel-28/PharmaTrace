How PharmaTrace Works
End-to-end cryptographic traceability lifecycle
1. Production 🏭
Certified Manufacturer

Enters lab composition, mfg/exp dates, and generates a cryptographically signed QR token.

2. Transit & Receipt 🚚
Distribution Checkpoint

Authorized logistics and wholesalers verify packaging seals and log receipt timestamps.

3. Retail Pharmacy 🏪
Licensed Pharmacist

Scans QR code on delivery, verifies integrity, places in stock, and marks as dispensed when sold.

4. Patient Verification 📱
End Customer

Scans package with any smartphone camera to see genuine provenance and instant recall alerts.
